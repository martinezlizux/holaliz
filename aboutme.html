My Journey

I’m a UX designer with a lifelong passion for artistic expression, storytelling, and solving meaningful problems. Ever since I was a kid—crayon in hand—I’ve been fascinated by color, form, and the way design shapes how we interact with the world.

Over the past 11+ years, I’ve focused on turning complex challenges into intuitive, user-centered solutions. My process combines UX design, research, and front-end development to build experiences that are thoughtful, functional, and human.

From mobile apps to large-scale enterprise tools, I love working closely with teams to co-create, iterate, and deliver work that makes an impact. As a mom of two amazing kids, I get to see the world through their eyes—something that constantly inspires me to stay curious, empathetic, and open to new ideas.
