Vasstel Point Brewing — Case Study

Role: Brand Designer & Illustrator
Focus: Illustration, storytelling, label system
Deliverables: Amber Lager label, color palette, mockups

Overview

Vasstel Point Brewing is a small-batch Mexican craft brewery inspired by local landscapes, outdoor culture, and traditional brewing methods. The goal of this project was to create a distinctive label for their Amber Lager, blending illustration, storytelling, and a handcrafted visual style that reflected the brand’s natural origin and artisanal character.

⸻

01. Context & Challenge

The brewery had a solid product and strong regional identity, but the visual presence didn’t yet communicate its uniqueness. The craft-beer shelf is crowded, and Vasstel Point needed a label capable of standing out while remaining authentic and hand-crafted.

The challenge: design an illustrated label that captured the brand’s spirit—nature, heritage, and craftsmanship—in a modern, memorable way.

⸻

02. Research & Inspiration

I researched illustration-driven labels from brands such as Samuel Adams, Modern Times, and Wainwright Golden Beer to define a strong direction.

Three creative pillars emerged:

  • Nature & Territory: use the surrounding mountains as a symbol of origin.
  • Iconic Symbolism: the deer represents strength, wilderness, and regional identity.
  • Hand-crafted Illustration: woodcut-inspired detail to reflect the brewing craft.

These references rooted the visual tone in outdoor culture, heritage, and small-batch brewing.

⸻

03. Concept Development

I explored multiple black-and-white sketches to define the deer’s personality, proportions, and how the brand name would integrate within the antlers. These drafts focused on composition, symmetry, and a unique voice for the label.

I also tested color studies—earthy browns, amber tones, forest greens, deep blues—to craft a palette aligned with craft-beer aesthetics and the brand’s natural inspiration.

⸻

04. Visual Design & Illustration

The final illustration uses hand-drawn lines and textured shading to evoke a traditional engraving aesthetic. Key elements include:

  • A front-facing deer as the hero.
  • Mountain landscapes inspired by local volcanic formations.
  • Hop and barley details highlighting the brewing process.
  • A hand-drawn banner to present the beer style (Amber Lager).
  • Custom lettering to keep the logo organic and cohesive.

This handcrafted approach reinforces authenticity and positions the beer as a premium, small-batch product.

⸻

05. Final Label & Mockups

The final label merges every illustrated element into a balanced composition. Mockups demonstrate:

  • Strong shelf visibility.
  • High contrast and readability.
  • A unique visual identity that scales to future beer styles.

The result is bold, natural, and distinctly artisanal—a strong reflection of the brand’s story and landscape.

Take Away

This project highlights my ability to combine illustration, branding, and visual storytelling to create products with personality and a clear narrative.

For Vasstel Point Brewing, illustration became the heart of the identity—transforming a local craft beer into a memorable and authentic brand experience.
