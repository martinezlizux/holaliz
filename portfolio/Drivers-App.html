Mobility & Logistics Product Redesign

BD Drivers App

Streamlining field operations: From paper-based chaos to a unified, real-time coordination experience.

Client BD Experience
Year 2024
Duration 2 Months
My Role Sole UX Designer

Overview

Service updates that once took up to 24 hours were reduced to minutes by redesigning how drivers, logistics, and operations coordinated in the field.

A leading travel service provider in Latin America relied on fragmented tools and paper-based processes that made real-time updates slow and unreliable. I led the redesign of the driver experience to align the product with real workflows, reduce operational friction, and improve traveler trust.

Project Objectives

  • Eliminate manual coordination calls
  • Increase digital adoption by 40%
  • Optimize field safety and security
  • Reduce operational reporting delay

Logistic Breakdown

Drivers operated in a high-stress environment where every second mattered. The existing app's failure to adapt to field reality forced coordination into unsafe, manual workarounds.

Operational Friction

  • Lagged Communication: 24-hour delays in service reporting due to paper-based logs.
  • Safety Risks: Drivers using multiple messaging apps while operating vehicles.
  • Workflow Mismatch: Original design didn't account for the "gloved hand" or "bright sun" field constraints.
  • Data Silos: Logistics had no visibility into real-time driver locations or status.

The Solution: A Frictionless Operations Hub

Replacing paper forms and chaos with an intelligent, workflow-aware web application.

Cross-Platform PWA

Eliminated the need for specialized hardware by deploying a responsive Progressive Web App that runs on any smartphone.

One-Tap Workflows

UI components designed for high-glare environments and one-handed operation, reducing distraction while on the road.

Real-Time Sync

Instant data synchronization between the vehicle and the logistics center, providing 100% visibility of the operation.

Safety-First Inputs

Replaced manual typing with smart selectors and automated status triggers to keep drivers' eyes on the road.
