From paper-based chaos to a unified, real-time coordination experience

Client BD Experience
Year 2024
Duration 2 Months
My Role UX Designer

Overview

Service updates that once took up to 24 hours were reduced to minutes by redesigning how drivers, logistics, and operations coordinated in the field. A leading travel service provider in Latin America relied on fragmented tools, paper-based processes, and phone calls that made real-time updates slow and unreliable. As the sole UX Designer embedded in the transportation operations team, I led the redesign of the driver experience to align the product with real workflows, reduce operational friction, and improve traveler trust.

Project Objectives

  • Reduce reliance on phone calls and manual coordination
  • Increase adoption of digital services
  • Improve accessibility for users of all ages
  • Implement enhanced security features

Logistic Breakdown

Drivers did not trust or rely on the existing app, shifting coordination to calls, paper forms, and messaging apps. This introduced delays, safety risks while driving, and an inconsistent experience for travelers.

Main Challenges

  • → Fragmented tools and manual processes increased operational complexity and costs
  • → Drivers were not involved in the original design, resulting in poor workflow fit
  • → Heavy reliance on calls and messages slowed decisions and introduced risk
  • → Siloed departments caused process misunderstandings and misaligned expectations
  • → Strict device security made logging actions slow and frustrating in the field

A Unified, Real-Time Driver Experience

The solution consolidated fragmented tools into a single, responsive web app designed around real driver workflows and operational needs.

Cross-Platform Web App

A responsive web app replaced multiple device-specific builds, reducing hardware and maintenance costs.

Workflow-Aligned Logging

Actions were structured to match the real sequence of driver tasks, enabling fast and consistent updates.

Low Cognitive Load Interface

Non-essential content was removed to minimize taps and distractions while driving.

Operationally Defined Real-Time Updates

Clear rules defined when updates were required, who needed visibility, and on which devices.

Operational Impact

The redesigned experience transformed a fragile, call-driven process into a faster, safer, and more reliable operation.
