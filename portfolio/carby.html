AI-Driven Health Experience

Carby: Intelligent Carb
Counting with AI

A frictionless PWA designed to radically simplify nutritional management for people with diabetes and athletes using real-time AI analysis.

The Challenge

Counting carbs shouldn't be a full-time job.

Traditional apps require searching through endless databases and weighing every single ingredient. For someone with diabetes, this tedium is a constant friction point that leads to error or abandonment.

The goal was to create a "frictionless" experience where users could log their meals in seconds using voice, photos, or natural language.

Key Focus Areas

  • Zero-friction data entry via Voice & Image
  • AI-powered nutritional estimation
  • PWA accessibility for quick access

The Process

From Concept to Deployment

1. Discovery & User Pain Points

Interviews with diabetic patients revealed that the "pre-meal mental load" — calculating carbs before eating — was the biggest stressor. The design responded by moving the calculation burden to the AI.

2. Information Architecture

We stripped away the "database search" model. Instead, the UI features a "Conversational Console" where users can speak or type: "A bowl of oatmeal with bananas and two strips of bacon."

3. Multi-modal Interface Design

Using Vision AI, the app analyzes plate photos to estimate portions. This serves as a secondary verification tool, corroborating voice input with visual data.

4. Implementation & Vibe Coding

Developed using a "Designer-Led AI" workflow, where structural components were scaffolded with AI while preserving strict UX guardrails and branding.
